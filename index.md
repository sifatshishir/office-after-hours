# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 28 October 2026*

This policy covers **Office Chaos Uploader**, the publishing tool behind the Facebook Page and YouTube channel **Office After Hours**.

## What the app is

Office Chaos Uploader is a private tool that only the owner of Office After Hours uses. It runs on the owner's own computer. It publishes the owner's own videos, with their titles, captions and thumbnails, to the owner's own Facebook Page and YouTube channel. It also reads view counts for those videos. No one else can sign in to it or use it.

## Information we collect

- **From visitors and viewers: nothing.** The app does not collect, store or process personal information about people who watch, like or comment on our videos.
- **From the owner:** the access tokens that Facebook and Google issue so the app can post to the owner's Page and channel. These are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared.
- **Video statistics:** totals such as plays, average watch time and reactions for our own videos. These are used only to decide what to make next.

## How information is used and shared

We use this information only to publish and schedule our own videos and to measure how they perform. We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Data retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the owner's computer until they are disconnected in the app or they expire.

To remove the app's access, open Facebook **Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations** (or Google **Account → Security → Third-party connections**) and remove the app. This deletes the app's access immediately.

If you think we hold any data about you and want it deleted, send a message to the **Office After Hours** Facebook Page. We will reply and delete it within 30 days.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
